# Closeout and Evidence

Launch is not complete until owner/provider gates are closed.

Current known launch gates from recent PR evidence include:
- point daffordablehomes.com at the new site;
- set and test lead webhooks;
- supply verified business facts;
- broker/compliance sign-off;
- approve final hero imagery;
- settle Sanity/content ownership;
- configure analytics;
- complete handover sign-off;
- remove duplicate/unused Vercel project if still present;
- verify Search Console/Bing and sitemap after cutover.

Record exact production SHA and deployment evidence after launch.

## Evidence standard
A closeout entry should identify the exact merge SHA, CI result, deployment identity, production verification, owner/external gates, and rollback/recovery path where applicable.
