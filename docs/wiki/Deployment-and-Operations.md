# Deployment and Operations

Operational checks include:
- Vercel project identity;
- custom-domain cutover;
- lead webhook configuration;
- business-fact configuration;
- Sanity/content ownership where applicable;
- Search Console and Bing verification;
- Google Business Profile;
- analytics;
- approved MLS/IDX provider;
- compliance/broker review.

Do not expose environment values. Record names, owners, locations, and verification steps only.
