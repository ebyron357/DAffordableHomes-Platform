## Asset acceptance rules

Every future image must include:

- ownership or license source
- photographer or generator record where applicable
- permitted commercial use
- page and placement
- original and optimized filename
- responsive dimensions
- crop and object-position guidance
- accurate alt text
- statement that the image is not a live listing when relevant
- review for distorted architecture, hands, vehicles, landscaping, signage, insignia, and false endorsements

No MLS, brokerage, Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, builder, photographer, or listing image may be copied without explicit permission.
