NACA PROGRAM GUIDE

Prepare before the property search.

NACA controls its counseling and qualification process. Debra supports the real-estate side of a purchase.

Responsible sequence

  1. Confirm current requirements with NACA.
  2. Complete counseling and document requests.
  3. Understand the approved payment and property requirements.
  4. Search within the current qualification.
  5. Coordinate inspection, negotiation, and closing.
